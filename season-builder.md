# THE SEASON BUILDER — HOW THE APP BUILDS YOUR TRAINING

**You answer a few questions in Settings. The app builds the whole season from them — Prep when there's no fight, a camp fitted to the date and format when there is, the transition after it — out of the two master programs, Optimal 8 · Prep and Optimal 8 · Camp. It invents nothing. Shorter camps are made by removing weeks in a fixed order; every option swaps in an alternative written on these pages. The proof is a test the app must pass: enter the March fight and it reproduces the camp exactly, week for week.**

---

## WHAT YOU ENTER

1. **Fight booked?** No or Yes.
2. **If no:** the app runs Prep. Optimal 8 Fighter stays in Settings as the classic, if you ever want it.
3. **If yes:**
   - **The date.** The app counts the weeks.
   - **The format:** rounds × minutes, e.g. 3 × 2 or 6 × 3.
   - **Rest between rounds:** 1 minute unless you change it.
   - **Weigh-in:** the day before, or on the day. It sets the fight-day food.
   - **How fit you are right now:** low, moderate or good. The first tests confirm or correct it.
   - **Emphasis:** none, power, strength or durability. One, or none.

Change any answer and the app re-plans from today, keeps everything you've logged, and tells you in one line what changed.

---

## WHAT THE APP DOES WITH IT

Weeks are counted in whole training weeks, Monday to Sunday, from the Monday the plan starts up to and including the fight week.

| Your answers | What runs |
|---|---|
| No fight | Prep — the 16-week cycle, on repeat |
| Fight 11 or more weeks away | Prep, fitted to the weeks before the camp, then the full 10-week camp |
| Fight 6–10 weeks away (7–10 from low fitness) | The camp, shortened from the front |
| Fight under 6 weeks away (under 7 from low fitness) | The short-notice camp |
| After the fight | The transition, then the next fight's plan, or Prep |

## THE CAMP — THE MASTER, AND HOW IT SHORTENS

**The master is ten weeks**, exactly as Optimal 8 · Camp writes them: **F** foundation · **B1 B2 B3** build · **E** easy + tests · **P1 P2 P3** peak (P3 is the last hard week) · **S** sharpen · **FW** fight week.

**From moderate or good fitness:**

| Weeks | The camp |
|---|---|
| 10 | F · B1 · B2 · B3 · E · P1 · P2 · P3 · S · FW |
| 9 | F · B2 · B3 · E · P1 · P2 · P3 · S · FW |
| 8 | F · B2 · E · P1 · P2 · P3 · S · FW |
| 7 | F · B2 · P1 · P2 · P3 · S · FW |
| 6 | F · B2 · P1 · P3 · S · FW |

**From low fitness** there are two foundation weeks — **F1** is week F at two-thirds dose, then F as written:

| Weeks | The camp |
|---|---|
| 10 | F1 · F · B2 · B3 · E · P1 · P2 · P3 · S · FW |
| 9 | F1 · F · B2 · E · P1 · P2 · P3 · S · FW |
| 8 | F1 · F · B2 · P1 · P2 · P3 · S · FW |
| 7 | F1 · F · B2 · P1 · P3 · S · FW |

**What never changes:** the sharpen week and fight week are always the last two; the peak block is never shorter than two weeks, and P3 — the last hard week — is always in it.

**How a kept week runs:** exactly as the master writes it — the same sessions, loads and order — with four exceptions: the rounds follow your fight's format (the Format section); the conditioning follows the format's pairs; the strength and power doses follow engine-first and your emphasis; and the tests run in week 1, in the easy week if it's kept, and in the sharpen week. With no easy week, the working weights carry through the camp unchanged. Repeat sled starts fall on the camp's even-numbered weeks, counted from its first week.

**Fight not on a Saturday:** fight week slides — the activation two days out, nothing the day before, the rehearsal six days out.

## THE FORMAT — ROUNDS, MINUTES, REST

**The rounds simulation** is your fight: your round length, your number of rounds, your rest. Each round splits into thirds — SkiErg, assault bike, then medicine-ball slams or the bag. That's a minute each for 3-minute rounds, forty seconds each for 2-minute rounds. The corner minute runs in every rest.

**How many rounds, week by week** — R is the number of rounds in your fight:

| Week | Rounds |
|---|---|
| F1 (low fitness) | R, at 90 seconds' rest — scored, the baseline |
| F | R, at fight rest — scored if it's the camp's first week |
| Build | R — engine-first: R + 1 |
| Easy + tests | R, scored |
| Peak, before the last hard week | R + 2 for fights of 5 rounds or more; R + 1 for fights of 4 or fewer — engine-first: one more |
| P3, the last hard week | **The ceiling.** 5 rounds or more: R plus two-thirds of R, rounded up, never more than R + 4 (6 → 10, 8 → 12, 10 → 14). 4 rounds or fewer: **the double** — R + 1 rounds, five minutes easy, then R more. Scored, the last read |
| Sharpen | The rehearsal: R rounds at fight time of day |
| Fight week | None |

**Scored weeks:** week 1, the last build week, the easy week if kept, and P3. The fade is the last round's output divided by the first.

**Thursday's rounds on the erg** (in the master's B3 and P2) run at your round length: R + 1 rounds in B3, R + 2 in P2.

**The sharpen week's fight-pace rounds** (Thursday): two-thirds of R, rounded up, never fewer than three — 6 → 4, 3 → 3.

**The conditioning pairs.** Total fight time is rounds × minutes.

- **10 minutes or more** (6 × 3 and the like): Tuesday and Thursday run exactly as the master writes them, week by week.
- **9 minutes or less** (3 × 2, 3 × 3, 4 × 2): the short format leans on repeat efforts.

| Week | Tuesday | Thursday |
|---|---|---|
| F1 / F | as the master's week 1: 4-minute intervals (burst test first in week 1) | as the master's week 1: tempo intervals (nasal test first in week 1) |
| Build | repeat bursts | 40-second repeats |
| Easy + tests | as the master | as the master |
| P1 | 4-minute intervals | repeat bursts |
| P2 | repeat bursts | 40-second repeats |
| P3 | 40-second repeats | repeat bursts |
| Sharpen | as the master | fight-pace rounds, as above |

**The check that this is right:** 6 × 3 at 1 minute's rest, ten weeks, no emphasis, gives every number in Optimal 8 · Camp — six rounds through the build, six in the easy week, eight in P1 and P2, ten in P3, six at the rehearsal, seven and eight rounds on the erg.

## STARTING FITNESS

| | Low | Moderate | Good |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | two weeks: F1 at two-thirds dose, then F | one week, F | one week, F |
| Engine-first | on | off | off |
| The tendon ramp | on | off | off |
| Working weights | checked before week 1 | checked before week 1, unless a test day was in the last three weeks | test day's numbers |

**Two-thirds dose (F1):**

- Every set count and interval count drops by a third, rounded down (3 sets → 2, 4 intervals → 2, 10 → 6). Base-ride and easy-session minutes drop by a third, rounded down to the nearest five (45 → 30, 40 → 25). The Nordics follow their own ramp instead.
- The interval sessions run at 90% effort — hard, not all-out.
- The rounds simulation runs at 90 seconds' rest.
- Loads stay as the week writes them; only the volume comes down.

**The tendon ramp (low fitness):**

- Flying sprints at 90% for the camp's first two weeks; flat out from week 3.
- No depth jumps before week 4 — loaded drop jumps until then.
- Nordics ramp 2 × 3, 2 × 4, 3 × 4, then the camp's numbers.

**The working-weight check:** on each of the squat, trap bar and bench press, work up to a set of 3 that's hard but leaves two more in you. That weight × 1.08 is the working max every percentage reads from.

**When a camp doesn't start straight after a Prep test week:** before week 1 if there's time, otherwise in week 1:

- The working-weight checks.
- The broad jump and the rotational throw.
- The 20-minute test, which sets your heart-rate zones.

**Straight after a Prep test week**, the app sets good fitness and uses test day's numbers.

**The tests correct the answer:** the first scored simulation's fade under 75% means low, 75–84% moderate, 85% or more good. If it disagrees with what you entered, the app says so in one line and adjusts from the next Monday.

## ENGINE-FIRST — FOR A BIG GAP

On automatically from low fitness, and in every short-notice camp. It doesn't add hard sessions; it makes the three you have bigger and gives them the time.

- **Strength to its hold dose.** Trap bar, squat and bench: two work sets, at the week's phase and load. Push press and chins: two sets. The contrast circuits: two rounds. Never lighter than the week says — only fewer sets.
- **Power to its hold dose.** Tuesday's jumps, Saturday's sprints and every throw stay as written; they're cheap and they keep the snap.
- **The engine gets the time:**
  - One round more in the build and peak simulations (the table above).
  - 40-second repeats always 2 × 6, and repeat bursts always 2 × 8.
  - The Wednesday easy thirty becomes forty.
  - Monday's base stays at its 45-minute cap.

## THE EMPHASIS — ONE LIGHT TILT, OR NONE

**The rules:**

- The engine is never touched: the three hard conditioning sessions and the base ride.
- One emphasis at a time.
- About 10–15 minutes a week moves.
- Nothing takes a lift below two work sets or a power drill below two sets.
- The emphasis runs from week 1 to the last hard week. The sharpen week and fight week run as written.
- It applies to camps only; Prep runs as written.

| Emphasis | Adds | Takes from |
|---|---|---|
| **None** | — | — |
| **Power** | One more round of Saturday's contrast circuit (one more set of reactive jumps in weeks without contrast); one more set of each of Thursday's throws; Tuesday's box jumps at 4 × 3 all camp | Trap bar and squat, one set each |
| **Strength** | One more set of trap bar, squat and bench press, at the top of the week's load (+2.5%) | Tuesday's broad jumps one set; Saturday's reactive jumps one set; the contrast circuit one round. In a camp under 8 weeks the app warns: "Strength takes longer than this camp has — power is the better pick." |
| **Durability** | Neck holds on Saturday after the squat (four neck days a week); the Spanish squat hold and the Achilles hold add 2.5 kg whenever the last hold was solid; one more set of Nordics, Copenhagen planks and suitcase carries; loaded drop jumps instead of depth jumps all camp, at the same sets and reps; the Edge's extra contacts don't apply | Side bounds one set; push press one set; broad jumps one set |

## YOUR STANDING RULES — EVERY PROGRAM, EVERY WEEK

1. **Three genuinely hard conditioning sessions a week at most** — Tuesday, Thursday, Sunday. Everything else easy.
2. **The tendon ramp** in every camp from low fitness, and **the tendon gate** every Sunday: Achilles or knee at 3 or more out of 10 means no depth jumps next week; hamstring at 3 or more means no flying sprints.
3. **Hydration is the rule, not a guide.** The sauna at most twice a week, never without the water.
4. **No dehydration cuts, ever.** If there's weight to make, it comes off through food, half a percent of bodyweight a week at most.
5. **Before every camp:** bloods — full blood count, lipids, liver and kidneys — and blood pressure. A resting ECG once a year. The app puts this in the week before the camp's first Monday.
6. **No maxes in camp. Friday sleeps. The strap gates every day**, and two yellow mornings in a week take the Edge off.
7. **The Edge** starts from the first peak or engine week in a camp from low fitness or at short notice, and the tendon ramp overrides it.

## PREP — WITH NO FIGHT, AND BEFORE A CAMP

Prep's fourteen weeks are **P1 to P14** as Optimal 8 · Prep writes them.

**With no fight: the 16-week cycle, on repeat.** The fourteen weeks plus two:

- **P4b** is P4 again at +2.5%.
- **P12b** is P12 again at 88%, depth jumps 4 × 4.

| Weeks 1–16 | P1 · P2 · P3 · P4 · P4b · P5 · P6 · P7 · P8 · P9 · P10 · P11 · P12 · P12b · P13 · P14 |
|---|---|

That's accumulate in weeks 1–6 (week 6 lighter, with tests), intensify in weeks 7–11 (max singles in week 10, week 11 easy with tests), convert in weeks 12–15, and the test week in week 16.

Test day is week 16's Saturday. The next cycle starts the Monday after, its working weights reset from test day. The size block runs full in weeks 1–6, at half dose in weeks 7–15, and not at all in week 16.

**How a fitted Prep runs:**

- Every kept week runs exactly as written.
- P14, the test week, always ends it, with test day on its Saturday and the camp on the Monday after.
- The bar-speed profiles are drawn in the Prep's first week and again in P10 if it's kept, otherwise in P14.
- If P6 isn't kept, the bench press starts in the first heavy week at P6's load, 80%.
- The size block runs full in kept weeks P1–P5, at half dose in the others, and not in P14.
- **The Christmas rule:** the week with 25 December in it runs at three-quarters volume, intensity kept.

## AFTER A FIGHT — THE TRANSITION

**How long:**

- One week after a fight of 9 minutes or less.
- Two weeks after a longer one.

**What it is:**

- The first three days: RANGE, the sit and walking.
- Then easy: base rides, the movement session, calisthenics at half sets, nothing above 70%, no sprints, no rounds.
- The morning after the fight, the one question from Iron Mind.

**It runs on until the strap says you're back:** resting heart rate within 3 beats of baseline and HRV within 10% of its average, three mornings running. Then the next fight's plan, or Prep.
