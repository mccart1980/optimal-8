# THE GUIDE — PLAIN ENGLISH

## WHAT TO DO TODAY
Open the app. TODAY is the whole day in one list, top to bottom, by the clock: the morning, the session, every feed, every drink, the site, the evening, bed. Each row has its time. The row you're on is open. Do it, press NEXT. That's the whole system. If you only ever read this page, read this page.

Type your session start at the top and every time below moves with it. On a day with no session, type when you woke.

## THE MORNING, IN ORDER
1. Strap on, lie still one minute. Type the two numbers from the strap app: resting heart rate and HRV.
   Then 500 ml of water — you lose about half a litre overnight.
2. Three big sighs — breathe in through the nose, sip in a bit more, long breath out through the mouth.
3. The one thing: what's the one job that makes today a win?
4. The five joint circles — the app times them.
5. Four yes/no taps. The app says GREEN, YELLOW or RED.
6. Half the bottle and a carb, twenty minutes before you start.

**GREEN** — do the session as written. **YELLOW** — the app has already taken 7% off your weights and told you to go easier on the bike; skip the last block. **RED** — warm-up, neck, hands, stretch at home. No lifting, no sprinting, no jumping today.

## THE WEIGHTS, WITHOUT THE MATHS
The app works out every weight for you. Load the bar to the number it shows.

- The **watch** measures how fast the bar moves. After your first work set, type the speed. The app tells you one of three things: **stay**, **take 5% off**, or **add 2.5%**. Do what it says.
- The watch will buzz when a rep gets too slow. **Rack it.** That set is finished, even if the app said five reps.
- A rep that grinds ends the set. Never fight one.
- **Max weeks** are marked. They're the only days you lift as heavy as you can, and only with the safety pins set.

## THE BIKE, WITHOUT THE JARGON
- **Easy** — you could talk. Nose breathing. Heart rate in the zone the app shows.
- **4-minute intervals** — hard for 4, easy for 3, four times.
- **40-second repeats** — flat out for 40, easy for 80, six times. The last three are meant to hurt.
- **Bursts** — flat out for 6–8 seconds, easy for 40, sixteen times in two sets.
- **Threshold** — the hardest pace you could hold for half an hour, twice for 8 minutes.
- **Rounds** — 3 minutes on, 1 minute off, six times. That's the fight.
- After every hard session: sit on the bike, eyes closed, one minute. Type your heart rate at the end of it.

## THE BLOCKS — WHAT THIS PART OF THE YEAR IS FOR
- **Build** (the first five or six weeks) — more reps, lighter weights, long slow lowering, longer easy rides, the size block, movement twice a week. You get bigger and fitter. It feels easy. It isn't.
- **Heavy** (the middle) — fewer reps, heavier bar, paused reps then fast reps, a max week near the end. You get strong.
- **Fast** (the last four) — heavy doubles straight into jumps, sprints at top speed, rounds at fight rest. Strength becomes speed.
- **Test week** — light, then test day. Every number gets measured and every weight resets.
- **Camp** — ten weeks to the fight. The app switches to it on the date you gave it.
- **After a fight** — two easy weeks. Don't skip them.

## LIGHTER WEEKS
The app marks them. Everything is lighter on purpose. Do not add anything. This is where the gains actually land.

## THE TESTS
They fall in the lighter weeks and on test day, and the app puts each one on the right day: bursts on the bike, the 20-minute ride, jump, throw, push-ups, chins, the breath hold, the seven bend-and-reach tests, the tape, the photos. Do them exactly as the row says. They're how the app decides your next weights and paces.

## AT HOME
Every evening: RANGE (the app times each stretch), the four holds and the handstand Monday to Thursday, the sit, the three questions, casein, bed by half past eight. Wednesday and Saturday evenings in the build weeks, and Saturdays after: the movement session — the app times it.

## FOOD AND DRINK
It's all on TODAY now, at its time, in the same list as everything else. Tick a feed when you've eaten it; tick a drink when it's gone. The top of TODAY adds it up: what you've eaten against the day, and the water against the target.

**Before you train.** Half the bottle and a carb twenty minutes before the session — a banana, raisins, rice cakes or dates. The other half the moment you finish. Breakfast a quarter of an hour after that, with 300 ml of water and the creatine in it.

**At work.** The litre bottle with a sachet at work start; it should be empty by nine. The two feeds at your two breaks, 500 ml with each. 300 ml mid-morning and mid-afternoon. Two bananas (or a swap) at three.

**The 5pm load.** Tuesday, Friday and Saturday — Monday and Thursday too in the build weeks. It's loading tomorrow's session, not filling you tonight. It never moves.

**Dinner and bed.** Dinner at its time with 300 ml. Casein half an hour before lights out on the nights dinner is early. The last big drink at eight — water at nine is a toilet trip at two.

**Swaps.** Open any feed and its matched options are underneath it. Pick one; the app remembers it for that day of the week. Every option hits the same numbers, so pick by what you can eat where you are — sat down or on the move.

**The urine check.** Ten and four. Pale straw, carry on. Dark: 500 ml now, a second sachet at three, salt on dinner.

**The phase.** The part of the year you're in sets the amounts. Build weeks add the extra loads. Easy weeks, the taper and fight week: don't cut. The two weeks after a fight eat a little less. Making weight is a switch in SETTINGS, only when the limit demands it — carbs off the light days, never protein, never the bottle, never the loads.

**Cooking.** COOK has the batch: cook the mince and the sweet potato in separate pans, weigh each one cooked, type the numbers in, and every batch feed shows what one portion looks like. SHOP is the weekly list, with anything your swaps need added.

**The referee.** Weigh in on Sundays, tape the arms and shoulders every four weeks — TRACK → REFEREE. Waist is the number that decides. Waist up faster than arms and shoulders: eat a little less. Nothing moving for six weeks: eat a little more. Weight falling: eat more — it's food, not the program.

## WORDS YOU'LL SEE
**Working weight** — the heaviest weight you could do the set with and still have two reps left. **Slow lowering** — five seconds down. **Paused** — stop dead at the bottom for three seconds. **Contrast** — a heavy double, then jumps. **Fade** — round six divided by round one; the number the whole thing is aimed at. **Recovery heart rate** — how far your heart rate drops in the minute after the last interval; higher is fitter. **HRV** — how much the gap between heartbeats varies; a bigger number than your usual means you're recovered, a smaller one means you're not. **BOLT** — how long you can comfortably hold your breath after a normal exhale. **Threshold** — the fastest pace you could keep for half an hour.

## IF SOMETHING'S WRONG
Elbow, wrist, knee or Achilles at 4 out of 10 or more on Sunday: the app drops the calisthenics to holds next week. A hamstring twinge on the sprint build-ups: no sprints that day, tell nobody you were going to do them anyway. Three yellow mornings in a row: the app makes it a red day. Don't argue with it.
