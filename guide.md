# THE GUIDE — PLAIN ENGLISH

## WHAT TO DO TODAY
Open the app. It shows one thing at a time. Do it, press NEXT. That's the whole system. If you only ever read this page, read this page.

## THE MORNING, IN ORDER
1. **Making weight?** Toilet, then the scales — before you eat or drink, nothing on. Type the number. Not making weight, the app doesn't show it.
2. Strap on, lie still one minute. Type the two numbers from the strap app: resting heart rate and HRV.
3. Three big sighs — breathe in through the nose, sip in a bit more, long breath out through the mouth.
4. The one thing: what's the one job that makes today a win?
5. The five joint circles — the app times them.
6. Four yes/no taps. The app says GREEN, YELLOW or RED.

**GREEN** — do the session as written. **YELLOW** — the app has already taken 7% off your weights and told you to go easier on the bike; skip the last block. **RED** — warm-up, neck, hands, stretch at home. No lifting, no sprinting, no jumping today.

## THE WEIGHTS, WITHOUT THE MATHS
The app works out every weight for you. Load the bar to the number it shows.

- The **watch** measures how fast the bar moves. After your first work set, type the speed. The app tells you to **stay** or **take 5% off**. It only **adds 2.5%** after two sessions in a row came up fast — one fast set can be the watch, not you. Do what it says.
- The watch will buzz when a rep gets too slow. **Rack it.** That set is finished, even if the app said five reps.
- A rep that grinds ends the set. Never fight one.
- **Top-triple weeks** are marked. You work up to one heavy set of three with a rep or two left in you, safety pins set. Never a true max.

## THE BIKE, WITHOUT THE JARGON
- **Easy** — you could talk. Nose breathing. Heart rate in the zone the app shows.
- **4-minute intervals** — hard for 4, easy for 3, four times.
- **40-second repeats** — flat out for 40, easy for 80, six times. The last three are meant to hurt.
- **Bursts** — flat out for 6–8 seconds, easy for 40, sixteen times in two sets.
- **Threshold** — the hardest pace you could hold for half an hour, twice for 8 minutes.
- **Rounds** — your fight, on the machines: your round length, your number of rounds, your rest. In the build and the peak you go past the fight on purpose — but if a round past the fight drops below three-quarters of your first, or your form goes, stop there.
- After every hard session: sit on the bike, eyes closed, one minute. Type your heart rate at the end of it.

## THE BLOCKS — WHAT THIS PART OF THE YEAR IS FOR
- **Build** (the first five or six weeks) — more reps, lighter weights, long slow lowering, longer easy rides, the size block at full dose (half dose after this block), movement twice a week. You get bigger and fitter. It feels easy. It isn't.
- **Heavy** (the middle) — fewer reps, heavier bar, paused reps then fast reps, a top-triple week near the end. You get strong.
- **Fast** (the last four) — heavy doubles straight into jumps, sprints at top speed, rounds at fight rest. Strength becomes speed.
- **Test week** — light, then test day. Every number gets measured and every weight resets.
- **Camp** — up to ten weeks to the fight, shorter if the fight is closer. The app builds it from what you enter under Fight booked? in Settings.
- **After a fight** — one easy week after a short fight, two after a long one, and until your morning numbers are back to normal. Don't skip it.

## SETTING A FIGHT
Settings → **Fight booked?**
- **No:** the app runs Prep, your everyday program, in 16-week cycles.
- **Yes:** type in the date, the rounds and minutes (like 3 × 2), the rest between rounds, when you weigh in (the day before in the morning, the day before later on, or on the day), how fit you are right now (low, moderate or good), and whether you want a little extra on power, strength or durability — or nothing extra.
- **A weight limit?** Put in the limit, any tolerance, and your natural weight — what you weigh after a normal week. The app runs your food down to it: the food guide's MAKING WEIGHT says how.

The app builds everything from that. The engine always comes first. Change anything and it re-plans from today, and tells you in one line what changed. Your first scored rounds tell the app how fit you really are, and it adjusts.

## LIGHTER WEEKS
The app marks them. Everything is lighter on purpose. Do not add anything. This is where the gains actually land.

## THE TESTS
They fall in the lighter weeks and on test day, and the app puts each one on the right day: bursts on the bike, the 20-minute ride, jump, throw, push-ups, chins, the breath hold, the seven bend-and-reach tests, the tape, the photos. Do them exactly as the row says. They're how the app decides your next weights and paces.

## AT HOME
Every evening: RANGE (the app times each stretch), the hollow and arch holds and the handstand on parallettes Monday to Thursday, the sit, the three questions, casein, bed by half past eight. Wednesday and Saturday evenings in the build weeks, and Saturdays after: the movement session — the app times it.

## FOOD AND DRINK
The food guide has the detail. Half the bottle and a banana twenty minutes before you train, the other half after. The 5pm feeds on Tuesday, Friday and Saturday are the ones that never move. Drink when the alarm says.

## WORDS YOU'LL SEE
**Working weight** — the heaviest weight you could do the set with and still have two reps left. **Slow lowering** — five seconds down. **Paused** — stop dead at the bottom for three seconds. **Contrast** — a heavy double, then jumps. **Fade** — round six divided by round one; the number the whole thing is aimed at. **Recovery heart rate** — how far your heart rate drops in the minute after the last interval; higher is fitter. **HRV** — how much the gap between heartbeats varies; a bigger number than your usual means you're recovered, a smaller one means you're not. **BOLT** — how long you can comfortably hold your breath after a normal exhale. **Threshold** — the fastest pace you could keep for half an hour. **Close and plant** — sprint five metres from your stance and stop dead in your stance. **Jump check** — three jumps on Tuesday that tell the app how fresh your legs are. **Peak-power load** — the weight for the bench throw and the trap bar jump where the watch shows the most power; the app works it out from the profile. **Engine-first** — when your fitness is low, the lifting drops to two sets so the conditioning gets the time; your strength holds on it. **Emphasis** — a little extra on one thing, never at the cost of the engine. **The double** — one round more than your fight, five minutes easy, then your fight's rounds again. **Top triple** — a heavy set of three with a rep or two left in you; the heaviest lifting in the program. **Drop landing** — step off a low box and land soft and still. **Low-box depth jump** — step off a low box and jump straight back up. **Seven-morning average** — your last seven morning weights averaged; when you're making weight, it's the only weight the app goes by.

## IF SOMETHING'S WRONG
**Tuesday's three jumps** more than 7% down on your average: the app counts it as a yellow. **Achilles or knee at 3 out of 10 or more on Sunday:** no jumping off boxes next week — the tendon holds carry on. **Hamstring at 3 or more:** no flying sprints next week, and Nordics only if they don't hurt. **Anything that hurts more than 3 out of 10 while you're doing it, or is worse the next morning:** stop it for that day. **Elbow or wrist at 4 or more:** the calisthenics go to holds next week. **Weight not coming off:** the Sunday check handles it — never water, never extra sessions. **A hamstring twinge on the sprint build-ups:** no sprints that day. **Three yellow mornings in a row:** the app makes it a red day. **After a fight:** stopped, dropped, or any headache, fogginess, dizziness or sensitivity to light — a doctor before any training. A bad or worsening headache, being sick, confusion or drowsiness — A&E, straight away. Don't argue with any of it.
