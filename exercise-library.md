# EXERCISE LIBRARY — WHAT EVERY ROW MEANS

Every exercise, session, test and move in the program, in two or three plain lines: what it is and how to do it. The app shows the entry on the card itself, under the name.

## WARM-UP AND GENERAL

**EASY BIKE (WARM-UP)** — Pedal easy for three minutes, just to get warm. Chatting pace.

**BAND PULL-APARTS** — Hold a light band at shoulder height, arms straight. Pull it apart until it touches your chest, squeezing your shoulder blades together, then let it back slowly.

**GOBLET SQUAT** — Hold a dumbbell upright against your chest. Sit down between your heels as deep as you can with your chest up, then stand.

**PUSH-UPS** — Hands under your shoulders, body straight from head to heels. Lower your chest to the floor and push back up.

**90/90 HIP SWITCHES** — Sit with both knees bent at right angles, one leg in front of you and one to the side behind you. Without using your hands, swivel both knees over to the other side, then back.

**POGO HOPS** — Small, fast bounces on the balls of your feet, legs almost straight, like skipping without a rope. Touch the floor as briefly as you can.

**SPRINT BUILD-UPS** — Three 20-metre runs getting faster: about 60%, 75% and 90% of top speed. Never skip them before sprinting — they protect your hamstrings.

**TURKISH GET-UP** — Lie on your back with a light kettlebell held straight up in one hand. Stand up step by step — onto your elbow, your hand, hips up, knee down, stand — then reverse back to the floor. The bell points at the ceiling the whole time.

**HIP AIRPLANES** — Stand on one leg holding a rack or chair. Tip your chest forward until it's level with the floor, then turn your chest down toward the floor and back up toward the ceiling, slowly.

**COSSACK SQUATS** — Feet very wide. Sit all the way down over one leg while the other stays straight with the toes up, then come back up and go to the other side.

**LEG SWINGS** — Hold a support. Swing one leg forward and back ten times, then across your body and out ten times. Relaxed, getting bigger.

**MEDICINE-BALL CHEST PASSES** — Ball at your chest, throw it hard against a wall with both hands, catch it, repeat.

**BIKE SPRINTS (10 SECONDS)** — Ten seconds flat out on the bike, a minute easy. Wakes the engine up before the rounds.

**WRIST CIRCLES** — Hands clasped or loose, circle your wrists ten times each way, slowly.

## POWER, JUMPS AND SPEED

**JUMP CHECK** — Three jumps straight up: hands on hips, a quick dip, jump as high as you can, land soft. Film them in slow motion on the jump app and log the best height. The app compares it to your average.

**BROAD JUMPS** — A two-footed jump forward as far as you can. Swing the arms, land soft, and hold the landing dead still before you stand up.

**BOX JUMPS** — Stand in front of a box about knee-to-hip height. Quick dip, jump onto it, land softly in a quarter squat, stand up, step down. Never jump down.

**SHUTTLE BURSTS** — Sprint five metres, plant your foot, turn and sprint back. That's one. Six of them with twenty seconds between. The hard turn is the exercise.

**STANCE STARTS** — Sprints from your boxing stance. Stand in your stance, then explode forward off your back foot and sprint ten metres flat out. Walk back and rest 90 seconds.

**CLOSE AND PLANT** — From your stance, sprint five metres, then stop dead back in your stance within one or two steps, balanced and ready to punch. Hold two seconds. Walk back, 60 seconds between.

**FLYING SPRINTS** — Jog and build up speed over 10–15 metres, then sprint the next 20 metres absolutely flat out. Walk back and rest fully, 2½–3 minutes. Also called flying 20s.

**LOADED DROP JUMPS** — Hold a hex dumbbell in each hand, 8–12 kg. Dip fast into a quarter squat, let the dumbbells drop straight down, and jump as high as you can empty-handed. Land soft and step away from the weights.

**DEPTH JUMPS** — Step off a 30–40 cm box — step, don't jump. The instant your feet touch the floor, jump straight up as high as you can. Spend as little time on the floor as possible.

**SIDE BOUNDS** — Stand on one leg, jump sideways as far as you can, land on the other leg and hold it dead still for two seconds. On 45° weeks, jump forward and across at an angle instead. In the fast weeks there's no hold — bounce straight back the other way.

**TRAP BAR JUMPS** — Stand inside a lightly loaded trap bar (the app gives the weight). Dip and jump as high as you can holding it, land soft, reset.

**BAND-ASSISTED JUMPS** — Loop a heavy band over the top of the rack and tuck it under your armpits so it pulls you upward. Jump as high and fast as you can — the band lets you move faster than you can on your own.

**PUSH PRESS** — Bar resting on the front of your shoulders. A quick, shallow dip of the knees, then drive the bar overhead with your legs and finish by pressing to straight arms. Lower it back to your shoulders under control.

## STRENGTH

**HEAVY SLED PUSH** — Load the sled heavy, lean into it at about 45 degrees and sprint it 20 metres, driving through your whole foot. Each run should take 5–7 seconds; faster, add weight; slower, take some off.

**REPEAT SLED STARTS** — The same heavy sled, but ten metres only, six times, with thirty seconds' rest. Short, hard, again.

**DRIVE HOLDS** — Load the sled so it can't move, or use a wall. In your boxing stance, drive into it as hard as you can for ten seconds through your back leg. Nothing moves; you push anyway.

**PUNCH-POSITION HOLD** — In your stance, a wrapped fist against a padded wall or a Smith-machine bar locked at shoulder height, arm almost straight, wrist dead straight. Punch into it as hard as you can for four seconds. Nothing moves.

**TRAP BAR DEADLIFT** — Stand inside the hexagonal bar, grip the handles, back flat, chest up. Drive the floor away and stand tall, then lower with control. Slow lowering: take five seconds to lower. Paused: stop an inch off the floor for three seconds. Fast: lift as fast as the bar will move.

**BACK SQUAT** — Bar across your upper back, feet about shoulder width. Sit down until your hips are just below your knees, then drive up hard. Safety pins set just below your lowest point, every set.

**BENCH PRESS** — Lie on the bench, feet flat, shoulder blades squeezed together. Lower the bar to your chest and press it back up. Paused: hold it still on your chest for two seconds. Pins at chest height, no collars.

**BENCH THROW** — On a Smith machine with a light bar: lower it to your chest, then press so hard the bar leaves your hands. Catch it and reset. No Smith machine: throw a 4–6 kg medicine ball off your chest at a wall.

**BENCH CONTRAST** — Two heavy bench presses, rest twenty seconds, then three bench throws. The heavy reps wake the muscles up so the throws go faster.

**SQUAT CONTRAST CIRCUIT** — Two heavy squats, rest 20 seconds, three box jumps, rest 20, three trap bar jumps, rest 20, three band-assisted jumps. Stop the round the moment your jumps get lower.

**CLUSTER SETS** — Two reps, rest the bar on the pins or the floor for twenty seconds, two more reps. That's one set. It lets you do more heavy reps at full speed.

**MAX SINGLE** — Work up to the heaviest single lift of the day in small jumps, following the app's ramp. Pins set, every time.

**SPLIT SQUAT, REAR FOOT ELEVATED** — Back foot up on a bench behind you, a dumbbell in each hand. Drop straight down until your back knee nearly touches the floor, then drive up through your front heel. Weaker leg first.

**WEIGHTED CHIN-UPS** — Palms facing away, extra weight on a belt or a dumbbell between your feet. From a dead hang, pull your chin over the bar, then lower slowly.

**NORDIC CURLS** — Kneel with your heels held under something solid. Keeping your body straight from knees to head, lower yourself forward as slowly as you can, catch yourself with your hands, push back up.

## CALISTHENICS

**RING ROWS** — Hang under rings set at hip height, body straight, heels on the floor. Pull your chest up to the rings, pause, lower slowly. Feet up on a box makes it harder; archer rows pull mostly with one arm.

**RING DIPS** — Hold yourself up on the rings with straight arms. Lower until your shoulders are level with your elbows, then press back up, turning your palms forward at the top. Easier versions: bar dips, then holding the top position.

**MUSCLE-UP LINE** — Chest-to-bar pull-ups (pull fast until the bar touches your chest), then muscle-up negatives (start above the bar and lower slowly through to a hang), building to the full muscle-up: pull up and over the bar to straight arms.

**PISTOL LINE** — The one-leg squat. Start with a box: stand on one leg, the other straight out in front, sit down to the box and stand up without the free foot touching. Then holding a post, then free.

**L-SIT LINE** — Hands on two boxes, arms straight, lift your body so your legs are held out in front of you. Start with knees tucked, then one leg straight, then both.

**TUCK FRONT LEVER** — Hang from a bar, pull your shoulder blades down, bring your knees to your chest, and lift your body until it's level with the floor, face up. Hold.

**HANDSTAND (ON PARALLETTES)** — Hands on the parallettes, kick up against a wall, body straight, wrists straight. Hold. Chest facing the wall is the stricter version; holds away from the wall come last.

**HOLLOW HOLD** — Lie on your back, lower back pressed into the floor, arms overhead, legs straight and lifted a few inches. Hold the banana shape.

**ARCH HOLD** — Lie face down and lift your arms, chest and legs off the floor. Hold.

## TRUNK, TENDONS, HANDS AND NECK

**PALLOF PRESS** — A band anchored at chest height beside you. Hold it at your chest, press your hands straight out and hold two seconds without letting the band twist you, then bring them back.

**AB WHEEL ROLLOUTS** — On your knees, roll the wheel forward only as far as your lower back stays flat, then pull it back.

**COPENHAGEN PLANK** — A side plank with your top foot on a bench and your bottom leg lifted off the floor. Hold. It works the inner thigh, the muscle you pivot off.

**HANGING LEG RAISES** — Hang from a bar and lift your legs to hip height or above without swinging, then lower slowly.

**SUITCASE CARRY** — A heavy dumbbell or kettlebell in one hand. Walk 30 metres standing tall without leaning to either side, then switch hands.

**SPANISH SQUAT HOLD** — A thick band around the back of both knees, anchored to the rack in front of you. Lean back into it so your shins stay upright and sit into a half squat. Hold dead still. It burns above the kneecap, never inside the knee.

**SEATED CALF RAISE** — Sitting, with weight on your knees from the machine or a padded barbell. Rise onto the balls of your feet, pause, lower slowly.

**ACHILLES HOLD** — On the edge of a step, holding dumbbells, rise onto your toes with straight knees and hold for 45 seconds.

**KNUCKLE HOLD** — Push-up position on your fists on a mat, wrists dead straight, forearm and knuckles in one line. Hold.

**BAND WRIST EXTENSION** — Forearm on your knee, palm down, the band in your hand and anchored under your foot. Lift your knuckles up toward you, then lower slowly.

**BAND DECELERATION CATCH** — A light band anchored behind you at shoulder height, the end in your hand, in your stance. Punch out fast; as the band snaps your arm back, brake it hard and stop it dead before it gets home. Fast out, hard stop.

**NECK FOUR-DIRECTION HOLDS** — Press your palm against your forehead and push your head into it as hard as you can for ten seconds; nothing moves. Then the back of your head, then each side.

**NECK RAPID TENSE** — A band resting light pressure on your head. Go from fully relaxed to fully braced in under a second, hold two seconds, relax. Each direction.

**NECK PERTURBATION HOLD** — A band around your head, anchored to the rack. Hold your neck still while you tug the band in small random pulses with your hand. Your head doesn't move.

**FACE PULLS** — A rope on a high cable, or a band on a door anchor. Pull it to your face with your elbows high, squeeze the backs of your shoulders, return slowly.

## THROWS

**ROTATIONAL SHOT-PUT THROW** — Medicine ball at your shoulder, side-on to a wall in your stance. Drive off your back hip and put the ball into the wall hard and flat, like a straight punch.

**DOWNWARD DIAGONAL THROW** — Ball held high outside your shoulder. Drive it down and across your body toward the opposite hip, into the wall low, your back foot pivoting — the overhand.

**HOOK THROW** — Ball at chest height in bent arms, side-on to the wall. Pivot hard off your lead leg and sling the ball sideways into the wall.

**LANDMINE PUNCH** — One end of a barbell in a corner or landmine sleeve, the other end at your shoulder, in your stance. Step in off your back foot and punch the bar up and away in one movement — never a slow press.

**MEDICINE-BALL SLAMS** — A 5–8 kg ball overhead; slam it into the floor as hard as you can, catch it on the bounce, go again without stopping.

## SIZE BLOCK

**LEAN-AWAY LATERAL RAISE** — Hold a rack with one hand and lean away from it. With the other hand, raise a dumbbell out to the side to shoulder height, then lower slowly.

**REAR-DELT FLY** — Bent over with a flat back, small dumbbells hanging. Raise them out to the sides, little fingers leading, squeeze, lower.

**CURLS** — EZ-bar or dumbbells, elbows still at your sides. Curl up, lower slowly to a full stretch. No swinging.

**HAMMER CURLS** — Dumbbells with your palms facing each other. Curl up, lower slowly.

**TRAP BAR SHRUGS** — Stand inside the trap bar, arms straight. Shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears, hold one or two seconds, lower slowly. Don't roll them.

## CONDITIONING

**EASY BASE (RIDE OR RUN)** — Steady and easy the whole way, breathing through your nose. If you can't talk in full sentences, slow down. Stay in the heart-rate zone the app shows.

**MODERATE INTERVALS** — Three minutes at a pace where you could still speak short sentences, three minutes easy. Four times.

**4-MINUTE INTERVALS** — Four minutes hard — you could say two or three words, no more — then three minutes easy. Four times.

**REPEAT BURSTS** — Six to eight seconds absolutely flat out, forty seconds easy, eight times. Five minutes easy, then eight more.

**40-SECOND REPEATS** — Forty seconds flat out, eighty seconds easy, six times. The last three are meant to hurt. Don't hold back early.

**TEMPO INTERVALS** — One minute at about 70% effort — hard, but never gasping — one minute easy. Ten times.

**THRESHOLD** — Eight minutes at the hardest pace you could hold for half an hour (short phrases only), three minutes easy, then eight minutes again.

**ROUNDS ON THE ERG** — Three-minute rounds on the bike or SkiErg at the pace you could hold for the whole fight, one minute's rest between. Log the first and the last round.

**FIGHT-PACE ROUNDS** — Four three-minute rounds at fight pace, one minute's rest. Sharp, then stop.

**THE ROUNDS (6 × 3 SIMULATION)** — Each three-minute round: minute one on the SkiErg, minute two on the assault bike, minute three ball slams (or the bag). One minute in your corner between rounds. Log round one and the last round.

**THE CORNER MINUTE** — Every rest between rounds: two big sighs the second the round ends (in through the nose, sip a little more on top, long slow breath out), then breathe through your nose, in for 3 and out for 6, standing, hands off your knees.

**THE 60-SECOND SETTLE** — Straight after the last interval: stay on the bike, eyes closed, and bring your breathing back to your nose. At 60 seconds, read your heart rate and type it in.

**THE POST-MAX SIT** — Straight after the last round: sit, eyes closed, and bring your breathing back to your nose. Note how many seconds it took.

**EASY HOUR / WEDNESDAY EASY THIRTY** — An easy walk, ride or run at chatting pace, breathing through your nose. Easy enough that it helps you recover.

**SAUNA** — 15–20 minutes. 500 ml of water before, 500 ml with a sachet after. Never straight into cold.

**SPEED MICRODOSE** — Fight-week sharpener, 25 minutes: warm-up, three 20-metre sprints at 90%, a few box jumps and throws. Fast, nothing tiring, done.

**ACTIVATION** — Two days before the fight, 20 minutes: warm-up, band work, three easy throws a side, two minutes of shadow boxing at pace, three big sighs. Done.

## MORNING AND EVENING

**THE MORNING FIVE** — Five slow joint circles on waking: neck, shoulders, mid-back (cat-camel, then elbow to the ceiling on all fours), hips (knee up, out, round and down), ankles. Three each way, about ten seconds a circle.

**FOAM ROLLER EXTENSIONS** — Lie with the roller across your mid-back, hands behind your head, and arch back over it eight times, moving it up a notch each time. Stay on the stiffest spot for 45 seconds, breathing out.

**OPEN BOOK** — Lie on your side, knees bent and stacked, arms together out in front. Open your top arm to the far side, chest following, knees staying down. Hold three seconds, come back.

**THREAD THE NEEDLE** — On all fours, slide one arm under the other, lowering that shoulder toward the floor. Hold three seconds.

**LOADED ROTATION (SIDE-LYING WINDMILL)** — On your side, knees bent, a light dumbbell held straight up in your top hand. Keeping your knees down, arc the weight over to the far side and back.

**90/90 STRETCH AND INTERNAL-ROTATION LIFT** — Sit in the 90/90 position and fold your chest over your front shin, breathing out. Then sit tall and lift your back foot off the floor without leaning, hold three seconds, lower.

**COUCH STRETCH** — Back foot up on the sofa behind you, knee down on a cushion, front foot forward. Squeeze your bottom and stand tall.

**DEEP SQUAT HOLD AND SQUAT-TO-STAND** — Sit in the very bottom of a squat holding a weight at your chest, elbows pushing your knees out, heels down. Then hold your toes, sink into the squat with your chest up, straighten your legs keeping hold, and sink again.

**HANG** — Hang from a bar, feet touching the floor as much as you need, shoulders relaxed up by your ears, breathing out.

**PRAYER STRETCH** — Kneel in front of a chair, elbows on the seat, hands behind your head, and let your chest sink toward the floor.

**SLEEPER STRETCH** — Lie on your side, bottom arm out in front bent at a right angle, forearm pointing up. With your other hand, gently press the forearm toward the floor.

**KETTLEBELL ARM BAR** — Lie on your back with a light kettlebell held straight up. Roll onto your side and then your front, keeping the bell pointing at the ceiling and your arm straight. Breathe there.

**WALL SLIDES** — Back flat against a wall, forearms on the wall like a goalpost. Slide them up and down without them leaving the wall.

**BAND EXTERNAL ROTATIONS** — Elbow tucked to your side, bent at a right angle, a band in your hand. Rotate your hand outward against the band, then back slowly.

**PIGEON STRETCH** — Front shin across the floor in front of you, back leg straight out behind, chest folding down over the shin, breathing out.

**OVERHEAD SQUAT HOLD** — A stick held wide overhead, arms straight. Sit to the bottom of a squat with the stick staying behind your ears. Hold.

**CAT-CAMEL** — On all fours, round your back up like a cat, then let it sag down. Slowly.

## TESTS

**THE 20-MINUTE TEST** — Five easy minutes, then as much distance as you can in 20 minutes on your usual bike or SkiErg. Log the distance and the highest heart rate you saw.

**BURST DECREMENT TEST** — Ten six-second flat-out bursts with thirty seconds easy between. The app compares the first burst with the last.

**NASAL THRESHOLD TEST** — Eight minutes breathing only through your nose, speeding up every two minutes until your mouth has to open. Log the pace where it opened.

**BROAD JUMP AND THROW TESTS** — Your best of three broad jumps, and your best of three rotational throws a side, measured.

**PUSH-UP AND CHIN-UP TESTS** — As many as you can: push-ups with your chest to a fist and two seconds down; chin-ups from a dead hang.

**PLANK AND COPENHAGEN TESTS** — Hold each as long as you can with good form. Log the time.

**BOLT** — Breathe normally, then breathe out normally, pinch your nose, and time how long until you feel the first clear urge to breathe.

**THE FOUR RANGE TESTS** — The app walks you through them: the 90/90 lift, the deep squat, the overhead reach against a wall, and hands behind your back.

**THE THREE FLEXIBILITY TESTS** — Knee to wall (how far your foot can be from the wall with your knee still touching and heel down), toe touch, and seated rotation with a stick.

**LOAD-VELOCITY PROFILE** — The main lift at four or five loads, two fast reps each, with the watch recording. The app draws your line and sets your bar-speed targets from it.

**TAPE, WEIGHT AND PHOTOS** — Weigh first thing; tape arms, shoulders and waist; photos front, side and back in the same light.
