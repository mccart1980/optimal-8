# THE FOOD GUIDE — PLAIN ENGLISH

## WHAT TO DO TODAY
Open the app. It shows today's feeds in order with a time on each. Eat what the row says at the time it says. Tap it. That's the whole system.

## THE DAY, IN ORDER
1. **Twenty minutes before you train:** half the bottle and a banana. Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday: stir 15 g of collagen into the bottle and have a small orange juice with it.
2. **Straight after:** the other half of the bottle, then the porridge from the thermos on the way to work.
3. **First break:** batch, or a wrap or bagel if you can't sit down.
4. **Lunch:** the same again.
5. **Three o'clock:** two bananas, or a bagel.
6. **Five o'clock, Tuesday, Friday and Saturday:** the top-up — a rice pouch, a banana and honey. **This is the one that never moves.** It's loading tomorrow's session.
7. **Dinner:** steak and eggs on the heavy days, chicken on the others.
8. **Before bed** on the early-dinner nights: casein in water.

Friday has no session, so no bottle — porridge when you wake, then the same day. Saturday and Sunday: porridge at half six, half the bottle at quarter to eight, the other half with two bananas straight after the session, then the feeds through the day.

## SIT DOWN OR ON THE MOVE
Every feed has a swap that matches it. Tap the feed to see them. Can't sit down at break? Wraps or a bagel instead of the tub. Can't face porridge? An egg bagel. The numbers hold whichever you pick. Pick by the slot, not by the day.

## THE PHASE — WHY THE APP SOMETIMES ADDS OR REMOVES A FEED
The training runs in blocks and the food follows it. The app knows which block you're in and changes the day for you:
- **Build** (the first weeks of Prep): an extra top-up on Monday and Thursday. You're being built; eat it.
- **Heavy, fast, camp, fight week:** the normal day.
- **Test week and the last two weeks before a fight:** the normal day, and **do not eat less** because you're training less. That's how people arrive empty.
- **The two weeks after a fight:** three of the 3pm feeds come off. Rest-week food.
- **Making weight:** when your fight has a weight limit. See MAKING WEIGHT below.

If a feed appears or disappears, that's why. Don't add anything back.

## THE BATCH
Cook the mince pot and the sweet potato separately. Weigh each once, cooked, in the COOK tab: type what went in raw and what it weighs cooked, and it tells you how many grams is one portion. Serve by those two numbers from then on.

## DRINKING
The alarms. **The litre bottle with a sachet at work start, empty by nine** — that's the one that matters. Five hundred with each feed, three hundred in the gaps, nothing big after eight. Check the colour at ten and four: pale straw is right; dark means drink now and a second sachet today.

## THE TAPE DECIDES
Weigh on Sunday. Tape every four to six weeks: arms, shoulders, waist. The app's Referee screen tells you if anything needs to change: waist up faster than arms means cut a banana; nothing moving with the waist flat means add a top-up; bodyweight falling more than half a kilo a week means eat more, whatever your appetite says. While you're making weight, the Sunday check runs the food instead and the half-a-kilo rule is off.

## MAKING WEIGHT
Only when your fight has a weight limit. In Settings, with the fight: the limit, any tolerance, your natural weight, and when you weigh in.

**Every morning:** toilet, then the scales — before you eat or drink, nothing on. Type the number. The app goes by the average of your last seven mornings, so one heavy morning means nothing.

**The line:** the app gives you an aim for every Sunday. The kilos above your natural weight come off first and fast; the rest is spread evenly, never more than about 1% of your weight a week.

**Every Sunday:** the app checks your average against the aim and sets next week's food — one step less, one step more, or the same. It never goes past Step 4.

**The steps take the carbs furthest from your hard sessions:** the three o'clock and the breakfast honey first; then half the rice or potato at lunch and at some dinners; then at mid-morning; then the breakfast banana. **The bottle, the 5pm loads, the protein and the drinking never change.** The food cards show exactly what's on the plate.

**Two Sundays before fight week:** if the app says you'll be over, it tells you straight. That's the time to talk to the promoter. Never water.

**Fight week, weighing in the day before:** your step until the two days before the weigh-in, then the light days — eggs, chicken, fish, rice cakes, a little white rice; no fruit, veg, beans, oats or wholemeal; drink the full schedule. Weigh-in day: the scales on waking; nothing before a morning weigh-in, small feeds before a later one; sip water.

**Over on the official scales?** Toilet, a walk, the re-weigh. Never a sauna, a sweat suit, spitting, pills or going without water.

**After the fight:** 2–4 kg back in a week is the food and water coming back, not fat.

## WORDS YOU'LL SEE
**Top-up** — the 5pm carb feed that loads tomorrow. **Batch / batch big** — the mince and sweet potato tub, standard or larger. **The bottle** — the UFIT 50 g, split around the session. **Casein** — slow protein before bed. **Surplus** — eating a bit more than you burn, on purpose, to build. **Phase** — which block of the training you're in. **Sachet** — the electrolytes. **Natural weight** — what you weigh after a normal week of eating and training. **Ceiling** — the limit plus the tolerance; the most the official scales can show. **Step** — how far the food is below the normal day, 0 to 4. **Light days** — the two days before a day-before weigh-in: low fibre, fewer carbs.

## FIGHT DAY
No water cuts, ever — any weight comes off through food, weeks before. Weighed in the day before? Drink a litre and a half for every kilo you're under your fight-week Sunday average (your normal morning weight if you weren't making weight), with a sachet in every litre, sipped. Eat plain carbs — rice, bagels, bananas, rice cakes and honey — every hour or two until three hours out. Three hours out: a pouch, chicken, a banana. One hour out: half the bottle and a banana. Nothing new on fight day. Caffeine an hour before the bell only if you've used it in camp and your blood pressure's normal.

## THREE RULES
1. The bottle round every session.
2. The 5pm loads never move.
3. Eat every feed, whether you're hungry or not. The plan is the plan; hunger isn't the referee, the tape is.
