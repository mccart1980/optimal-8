# THE HOME ROUTINES — CARD BY CARD

**Exactly what every card says in the morning and evening routines: the morning, RANGE, the fight-week RANGE, the Keep, the movement session and the skill block. The moves, the order and the holds are the ones the Prep and Camp documents write. They're laid out here one card at a time, in plain words, so nothing is guessed.**

---

## HOW THE CARDS WORK

- **One thing per card.** The side is in the card's name: "right arm", "left knee down", "right leg in front".
- **HOLD cards have a clock.** When the card comes up you get 8 seconds to get into position (GET SET), then the clock starts. When it runs out the phone buzzes and the next card comes up by itself. A card that says "at halfway" buzzes once at the halfway point.
- **REPS cards have no clock.** Do them at your own pace and tap DONE. A card never shows both a rep count and a clock.
- **◀ goes back a card, ▶ goes forward.** Nothing you've done is lost by going back.
- **"Same position"** means you stay exactly where the card before put you.

## THE MORNING

**THREE BIG SIGHS** · 3 sighs, about 30 seconds
Breathe in through your nose, then sip in a little more air on top, then let it all out slowly through your mouth. Three times.

**THE MORNING FIVE** · one card, five rows: tick each row as you finish it
Slowly: about ten seconds a circle. If something wants to cheat (a shrug, a lean, a bent knee), slow down there.

1. **Neck** — 3 slow circles each way, gentle, chin leading.
2. **Shoulders** — one arm at a time, arm straight. Draw the biggest circle you can: forward, up past your ear, back and down. 3 each way, each arm. Body still, no shrug.
3. **Mid-back** — on all fours. Round your back up like a cat, then let it sag, × 8. Then a hand behind your head and turn that elbow up to the ceiling, × 6 each side.
4. **Hips** — hold a wall, stand tall. Lift one knee to your chest, take it out to the side, round behind you and down. 3 each way, each leg.
5. **Ankles** — circles × 5 each way, each ankle. Then 10 slow, deep knee-bends over your toes, heels down.

## RANGE · every evening · about 20 minutes · the first twelve weeks

### Settle

**1 · SETTLE** · HOLD 60 s
Lie on your back, calves up on a chair. Five slow breaths: in through your nose for a count of 4, out for a count of 8.

### Mid-back

**2 · FOAM ROLLER — ARCH OVER IT** · REPS 8
Sit on the floor with the roller behind you and lie back so it's across your mid-back, hands behind your head. Arch back over it, then sit up a little and move the roller an inch higher. 8 times, working up your back. Notice where it feels stiffest.

**3 · FOAM ROLLER — THE STIFFEST SPOT** · HOLD 45 s
Put the roller back under the spot that felt stiffest. Arch back over it and stay there, breathing out slowly.

**4 · OPEN BOOK — RIGHT ARM** · HOLD 70 s
Lie on your left side, knees bent and stacked, both arms straight out in front. Open your right arm over to the other side and let your chest follow, knees staying down. Hold 3 seconds, bring it back, and keep repeating. At halfway, at the end of an opening: press your right hand up toward the ceiling (against nothing) for 5 seconds, relax, and open further.

**5 · OPEN BOOK — LEFT ARM** · HOLD 70 s
Lie on your right side, knees bent and stacked, both arms out in front. Open your left arm over to the other side and let your chest follow, knees down. Hold 3 seconds, come back, and keep repeating. At halfway: press your left hand toward the ceiling for 5 seconds, relax, and open further.

**6 · THREAD THE NEEDLE** · REPS 8 EACH SIDE
On all fours. Slide your right arm under your left arm along the floor until your right shoulder is down near the floor. Hold 3 seconds, come back. 8 times, then 8 with the left arm.

**7 · LOADED ROTATION** · REPS 6 EACH SIDE
Lie on your left side, knees bent, a light dumbbell held straight up in your right hand. Keeping your knees down, take the weight over to the other side in an arc, then back. 6 times, then lie on your right side and do 6 with your left hand.

### Hips

**8 · 90/90 — RIGHT LEG IN FRONT** · HOLD 45 s
Sit on the floor. Right leg in front of you, knee bent to a right angle, shin across your body. Left leg out to the side behind you, knee bent to a right angle. Both sitting bones on the floor. Fold your chest down over your right shin, breathing out slowly.

**9 · 90/90 — RIGHT LEG IN FRONT: PUSH AND RELAX** · REPS 3
Same position. Press your right shin down into the floor for 5 seconds, relax, then fold a little further. 3 times.

**10 · 90/90 — LIFT YOUR LEFT FOOT** · REPS 8
Same position; sit up tall. Lift your left foot (the one behind you) off the floor without leaning. Hold 3 seconds, lower. 8 times. It barely moves at first, and that's normal.

**11 · 90/90 — LEFT LEG IN FRONT** · HOLD 45 s
Swap over: left leg in front, knee bent to a right angle, shin across your body. Right leg out to the side behind you, knee bent to a right angle. Both sitting bones down. Fold your chest down over your left shin, breathing out slowly.

**12 · 90/90 — LEFT LEG IN FRONT: PUSH AND RELAX** · REPS 3
Same position. Press your left shin down into the floor for 5 seconds, relax, then fold a little further. 3 times.

**13 · 90/90 — LIFT YOUR RIGHT FOOT** · REPS 8
Same position; sit up tall. Lift your right foot (the one behind you) off the floor without leaning. Hold 3 seconds, lower. 8 times.

**14 · COUCH STRETCH — RIGHT KNEE DOWN** · HOLD 70 s
Kneel with your right knee on a cushion against the sofa, right foot up on the sofa behind you, left foot flat on the floor in front. Squeeze your right buttock and lift your chest tall. At halfway: drive your right knee down into the cushion for 5 seconds, relax, and lift taller.

**15 · COUCH STRETCH — LEFT KNEE DOWN** · HOLD 70 s
Left knee on the cushion, left foot up on the sofa behind you, right foot flat in front. Squeeze your left buttock and lift your chest tall. At halfway: drive your left knee down for 5 seconds, relax, and lift taller.

**16 · COSSACK SQUATS** · REPS 6 EACH SIDE
Feet very wide. Sit all the way down over your right leg, keeping your left leg straight with the heel on the floor and toes up. Pause 3 seconds at the bottom, come back up, then go down over the left leg. 6 each side, alternating.

**17 · DEEP SQUAT HOLD** · HOLD 70 s
Hold a weight at your chest and sit right down into the bottom of a squat, heels on the floor. Use your elbows to push your knees out. Breathe out slowly.

**18 · SQUAT-TO-STAND** · REPS 5
Stand, bend forward and hold your toes. Pull yourself down into a full squat with your chest up. Then straighten your legs, still holding your toes. That's one. 5 times.

**19 · HIP AIRPLANES** · REPS 5 EACH SIDE
Stand on your right leg, holding a chair with your left hand. Tip your chest forward until it's level with the floor. Slowly turn your chest down to face the floor, then open it up toward the ceiling. 5 times, then stand on your left leg.

### Shoulders

**20 · HANG** · HOLD 70 s
Hang from the bar with your feet on the floor as much as you need, shoulders relaxed up by your ears, breathing out. No bar: put your hands on a table, step back, bend at the hips and let your chest sink between your arms.

**21 · PRAYER STRETCH** · HOLD 70 s
Kneel in front of a chair, elbows on the seat, hands behind your head. Let your chest sink toward the floor. At halfway: press your elbows into the seat for 5 seconds, relax, and sink further.

**22 · SLEEPER STRETCH — RIGHT SHOULDER** · HOLD 45 s
Lie on your right side. Right arm straight out in front at shoulder height, elbow bent to a right angle, forearm pointing at the ceiling. With your left hand, gently press your right forearm down toward the floor. At halfway: push the forearm up into your hand for 5 seconds, relax, and press a little further.

**23 · SLEEPER STRETCH — LEFT SHOULDER** · HOLD 45 s
Lie on your left side, left arm out in front at shoulder height, elbow bent, forearm pointing up. With your right hand, gently press your left forearm toward the floor. At halfway: push up into your hand for 5 seconds, relax, and press further.

**24 · KETTLEBELL ARM BAR — RIGHT ARM** · HOLD 35 s
Lie on your back, a light kettlebell pressed straight up in your right hand, right knee bent. Roll onto your left side and slowly straighten your right leg behind you until you're lying on your front. The bell keeps pointing at the ceiling, arm straight, shoulder pulled down away from your ear. Breathe there.

**25 · KETTLEBELL ARM BAR — LEFT ARM** · HOLD 35 s
On your back, the bell pressed up in your left hand, left knee bent. Roll onto your right side and straighten your left leg behind you until you're on your front. The bell points at the ceiling, arm straight, shoulder down. Breathe there.

**26 · WALL SLIDES** · REPS 12
Back flat against a wall, forearms on the wall like a goalpost. Slide your arms up without them leaving the wall, reach for 3 seconds at the top, and slide back down. 12 times.

**27 · BAND EXTERNAL ROTATIONS** · REPS 15 EACH ARM
The band tied to something at waist height on your left. Right elbow tucked into your side, bent to a right angle, the band in your right hand. Turn your hand outward against the band, then back slowly. 15 times, then turn round and do 15 with the left arm.

## THE MOVEMENT SESSION · about 30 minutes · the evenings the program puts it in

**1 · SETTLE** · HOLD 2:00
Lie on your back, calves up on a chair. Slow breaths: in through your nose for a count of 4, out for a count of 8.

**2 · SHOULDER CIRCLES WITH A KETTLEBELL — RIGHT ARM** · REPS 3 EACH WAY
A 4–8 kg kettlebell in your right hand, arm straight. Draw the biggest circle you can (forward, up past your ear, back and down), slowly, body still. 3 forwards, 3 backwards.

**3 · SHOULDER CIRCLES WITH A KETTLEBELL — LEFT ARM** · REPS 3 EACH WAY
The bell in your left hand, arm straight. 3 forwards, 3 backwards, slowly, body still.

**4 · HIP CIRCLES — RIGHT LEG** · REPS 3 EACH WAY
Stand on your left leg, holding something if you need to. Right knee up, out to the side, round behind you and down. 3 each way.

**5 · HIP CIRCLES — LEFT LEG** · REPS 3 EACH WAY
Stand on your right leg. Left knee up, out, round behind and down. 3 each way.

**6 · CAT-CAMEL** · REPS 10
On all fours. Round your back up like a cat, then let it sag down. Slowly.

**7 · ELBOW TO THE CEILING** · REPS 8 EACH SIDE
On all fours, right hand behind your head. Turn your chest and point your right elbow up to the ceiling, then back down. 8 times, then the left.

**8 · THE GET-UP, SLOW** · REPS 2 EACH SIDE
Lie on your back, a light kettlebell pressed straight up in your right hand. Stand up step by step: onto your elbow, onto your hand, hips up, sweep the back leg through to kneel, then stand. Then go back down the same way. Hold every position for 5 seconds, and the bell points at the ceiling the whole time. 2 with the right hand, then 2 with the left.

**9 · DEEP SQUAT HOLD** · HOLD 60 s
Sit right down into the bottom of a squat, heels on the floor, elbows pushing your knees out. Breathe out slowly.

**10 · SQUAT-TO-STAND** · REPS 6
Stand, bend forward and hold your toes. Pull yourself down into a full squat with your chest up, then straighten your legs, still holding your toes. 6 times.

**11 · COSSACK SQUATS** · REPS 6 EACH SIDE
Feet very wide. Sit down over one leg, the other straight with the heel on the floor and toes up. Pause 3 seconds at the bottom. 6 each side, alternating.

**12 · OVERHEAD SQUAT HOLD — 1 OF 3** · HOLD 20 s
A stick held wide above your head, arms straight. Sit down to the bottom of a squat, keeping the stick behind your ears. Hold.

**13 · OVERHEAD SQUAT HOLD — 2 OF 3** · HOLD 20 s
Same again.

**14 · OVERHEAD SQUAT HOLD — 3 OF 3** · HOLD 20 s
Same again.

**15 · 90/90 SWITCHES** · REPS 5 EACH WAY
Sit in the 90/90: one leg in front, one out to the side behind, both knees bent to right angles. Without using your hands, swing both knees over to the other side, then back. 5 each way.

**16 · 90/90 — RIGHT LEG IN FRONT: LIFT YOUR LEFT FOOT** · REPS 8
Right leg in front, left leg behind, sitting tall. Lift your left foot off the floor without leaning, hold 3 seconds, lower. 8 times.

**17 · 90/90 — LEFT LEG IN FRONT: LIFT YOUR RIGHT FOOT** · REPS 8
Left leg in front, right leg behind, sitting tall. Lift your right foot without leaning, hold 3 seconds, lower. 8 times.

**18 · PIGEON — RIGHT LEG IN FRONT** · HOLD 60 s
Right shin across the floor in front of you, left leg straight out behind. Fold your chest down over your right shin, breathing out.

**19 · PIGEON — LEFT LEG IN FRONT** · HOLD 60 s
Left shin across the floor in front of you, right leg straight out behind. Fold your chest down over your left shin, breathing out.

**20 · HIP AIRPLANES** · REPS 5 EACH SIDE
Stand on your right leg, holding a chair with your left hand. Tip your chest forward until it's level with the floor. Slowly turn your chest down to face the floor, then open it up toward the ceiling. 5 times, then stand on your left leg.

**21 · HANG** · HOLD 45 s
Hang from the bar, feet on the floor as much as you need, shoulders relaxed up by your ears, breathing out.

**22 · KETTLEBELL ARM BAR — RIGHT ARM** · HOLD 30 s
As RANGE card 24.

**23 · KETTLEBELL ARM BAR — LEFT ARM** · HOLD 30 s
As RANGE card 25.

**24 · BAND PULL-APARTS** · REPS 20
A light band held at shoulder height, arms straight. Pull it apart until it touches your chest, squeezing your shoulder blades together, then let it back slowly.

**25 · BAND EXTERNAL ROTATIONS** · REPS 15 EACH ARM
As RANGE card 27.

**26 · STAND TALL** · HOLD 60 s
Stand tall with your eyes closed and notice where your body is.

## THE SKILL BLOCK · Monday to Thursday evenings · 5 minutes

**1 · WRIST CIRCLES** · REPS 10 EACH WAY
Hands clasped or loose, circle your wrists slowly, 10 times each way.

**2 · HANDSTAND ON PARALLETTES** · HOLD 3:00 (practice time)
Your level is shown on the card. Hands round the parallette bars, wrists straight: the way your wrist is when a punch lands. Body in one straight line. Hold, come down, rest, and go again until the 3 minutes are up.

**3 · HOLLOW HOLD — 1 OF 2** · HOLD 20 s
On your back, lower back pressed into the floor, arms overhead, legs straight and lifted a few inches. Hold the banana shape.

**4 · HOLLOW HOLD — 2 OF 2** · HOLD 20 s
Same again.

**5 · ARCH HOLD — 1 OF 2** · HOLD 20 s
Face down. Lift your arms, chest and legs off the floor. Hold.

**6 · ARCH HOLD — 2 OF 2** · HOLD 20 s
Same again.
