# MAKING WEIGHT — OPTIMAL 8

**When a fight has a weight limit, the app runs your food down to it. You put in the limit and step on the scales each morning. Every Sunday the app compares your week with the plan and moves the food one step down, one step up, or leaves it. The weight comes off through food only: never water, never extra training. The sessions don't change.**

---

## WHAT YOU ENTER

Settings → **Fight booked?** → Yes, with the fight's other answers:

- **Weight limit** — the number on the contract, in kilos. Leave it blank and the weight plan is off.
- **Tolerance** — how much over the limit the show allows. 0 if none.
- **Natural weight** — what you weigh after a normal week of eating and training. Leave it blank if you don't know.
- **Weigh-in** — the day before, in the morning (before 12) · the day before, in the afternoon or evening · on the day. Not confirmed yet? Pick afternoon or evening. It's the safer plan; change it when you know.

Change any of them and the app redraws the plan from your latest average. Your step stays where it is.

## EVERY MORNING — THE SCALES

Go to the toilet, then get on the scales before you eat or drink, with nothing on. Use the same scales, in the same spot, on a hard floor. Type the number. Miss a morning and nothing breaks, but three mornings a week is the least that works.

The app always uses the **average of your last seven mornings**, never one morning on its own. One salty dinner can put a kilo on overnight, and it's gone the next day.

## THE NUMBERS

**The ceiling** is the limit plus the tolerance: the most the official scales can read. A 76 limit with 1 kg tolerance gives a ceiling of 77.0.

| Weigh-in | Fight-week aim — your average on the Sunday before fight week | Weigh-in morning aim — on waking |
|---|---|---|
| The day before, morning | ceiling + 1.0 | ceiling − 0.5 |
| The day before, afternoon or evening | ceiling + 0.5 | ceiling − 1.0 |
| On the day | the ceiling | ceiling − 0.5 |

**Why the gap.** Fight week takes some weight off with food alone:

- **The two light days before a day-before weigh-in** take about 1 to 2 kg. That's less in your gut, plus less stored carbohydrate and the water it holds.
- **Two low-fibre days before an on-the-day weigh-in** take about half a kilo to a kilo.
- **An afternoon or evening weigh-in** needs an extra half kilo of room on waking, for the day's small feeds and drinks.
- **The half kilo under the ceiling** covers the difference between your scales and theirs.

Hit the fight-week aim and the official scales will read about **one kilo under the ceiling**, give or take half a kilo.

**Check the scales.** If you can, stand on the show's scales, or a gym's calibrated scales, in the sharpen week. If theirs read heavier than yours, aim lower by the difference.

## THE LINE — AN AIM FOR EVERY SUNDAY

The app draws a line from your weight now to the fight-week aim, with an aim for every Sunday in between.

- **The kilos above your natural weight come off first and fast.** They're extra food, water and stored carbohydrate, not fat. On the plan they drop about a kilo and a half a week, and the line expects that. Up to 4% of your weight counts this way; anything beyond that is real weight.
- **The rest, the real weight, is shared out evenly** over every week to the fight.
- **The speed limit.** The real weight never comes off faster than 1% of your natural weight a week, which is 0.8 kg at 80. Faster than that and it starts coming out of the engine and the muscle.
- **If the aim can't be reached inside the speed limit,** the line runs at the limit. The app tells you on day one what you'd weigh, so the call to the promoter happens now, not in fight week.

**Started from one morning's weight?** Then the first week is a settling week. The first Sunday's seven-morning average becomes the real start, and the line is redrawn from it.

## THE STEPS — HOW THE FOOD CHANGES

Step 0 is the menu as printed. Each step takes about 200–250 calories a day off, from the feeds furthest from your hard sessions first. **Each step includes everything in the steps before it.**

| Step | Day average | What changes |
|--|--|------------|
| **0** | ~3,600 | Nothing. The menu as printed. |
| **1** | ~3,350 | **No three o'clock.** No honey at breakfast. |
| **2** | ~3,150 | **Lunch: half the carb.** Thursday and Sunday dinner: half the carb. Saturday's mid-morning and lunch at standard portions, not BIG. |
| **3** | ~2,900 | **Mid-morning: half the carb.** Tuesday, Friday and Saturday dinner: half the carb, because the 5pm load has already done the job. Monday and Friday breakfast: no banana. |
| **4 · the floor** | ~2,700 | **Breakfast: no banana, every day.** Monday and Friday breakfast: half the oats or half the bagel as well. The BIG breakfasts at standard size. **Nothing goes lower than this.** |

**What never changes, at any step:**

- The bottle around every session.
- The 5pm loads.
- The protein in every feed.
- The weekend feed after the session.
- Monday's and Wednesday's dinner, carbs and all. They load Tuesday's and Thursday's hard sessions.
- The bedtime casein and the collagen. The creatine too, unless you choose the checkpoint's lever.
- The drinking schedule.

**While you're making weight,** the build block's extra Monday and Thursday loads don't run. The Referee's half-a-kilo rule is off too, because the Sunday check does that job.

## THE SUNDAY CHECK

On the weekly check, after the rounds, the app takes your seven-morning average, compares it with this Sunday's aim and sets next week's step. It changes by one step at most, and the new step starts on Monday.

| What it sees | Next week |
|----|------|
| **BEHIND** — more than 0.4 kg over the aim | **One step down** (less food). It holds instead if you had three or more yellow or red mornings this week (recovery comes first), if the weight is already coming off fast below your natural weight, or if you're already at Step 4. |
| **TOO FAST** — at or under your natural weight, the average dropped more than 1% of your natural weight this week | **One step up** (more food). |
| **AHEAD** — more than 0.8 kg under the aim, at or under your natural weight | **One step up.** |
| **ON TRACK** — anything else | The same step. |

**No step up in the plan's first two weeks.** The first drop is water and food, not fat.

Every check also shows **the forecast**: what the official scales will read if you stay on the line from here.

**The first step** comes from how fast the real weight has to come off: up to 0.35 kg a week is Step 1, up to 0.55 is Step 2, up to 0.75 is Step 3, and anything faster is Step 4. If there's nothing to lose, it's Step 0. If you only have kilos above your natural weight to lose, it's Step 1.

## THE CHECKPOINT — TWO SUNDAYS BEFORE FIGHT WEEK

If the forecast is over the ceiling, the app says it plainly: **"At this pace you'll be about X on the scales — Y over."** Two weeks out is the time to talk to the promoter about a catchweight, the next weight up, or a later date.

**One more lever if you're going for it, and still no water: stop the creatine from that Sunday.** The extra water it holds in the muscle drains slowly, about half a kilo by the weigh-in. It costs a little repeat power. Your call.

The plan carries on either way. The same card shows on the day you set the plan if the forecast is already over the ceiling.

## FIGHT WEEK

**The Sunday before fight week is the last check.** Nothing changes. It tells you where you are and what the week looks like.

### Weighing in the day before

- **From Monday up to the light days:** your last step.
- **The two days before the weigh-in day are THE LIGHT DAYS:** low fibre and fewer carbs. This menu replaces the whole day:

| Feed | What | kcal · P · C · F |
|---|-----|--|
| BEFORE AND AFTER (session days only) | Half the bottle each time, no carb | 144 · 25 · 9 · 0 each |
| BREAKFAST | 3 eggs on a white bagel | 445 · 28 · 46 · 17 |
| MID-MORNING | 150 g cooked chicken, prawns or a tin of tuna, 2 rice cakes | about 300 · 45 · 16 · 6 |
| LUNCH | 150 g cod or prawns, half a rice pouch | 330 · 38 · 36 · 3 |
| DINNER | 250 g chicken breast or cod, 2 eggs, half a rice pouch | about 625 · 76 · 37 · 16 |
| BEDTIME | Casein in water | 130 · 30 · 3 · 1 |

- No three o'clock and no 5pm load.
- No fruit, no veg, no beans, no oats, no wholemeal, no mushrooms, no passata.
- Salt as normal.
- **Drink the full schedule. Drinking less is the one thing that doesn't happen.**
- Creatine as normal. On a tendon day, collagen in water, without the orange juice.

**The weigh-in day:**

- Wake, toilet, scales. The app checks the number against your weigh-in morning aim.
- **Weigh-in in the morning:** nothing to eat until you've weighed in. Sip water to thirst. Take your after-weigh-in food and the bottle with you.
- **Weigh-in in the afternoon or evening:** small low-fibre feeds every three hours, the last one two hours before you weigh in:
  - 2 eggs and a rice cake
  - half the bottle
  - 100 g chicken or a tin of tuna

  Sip water through the day, about 250 ml an hour.
- **Over the morning aim:** halve the small feeds and keep sipping.
- **Still over on the official scales:** most shows give you a re-weigh within an hour or two. Use the toilet and walk in normal clothes. **Never a sauna, a sweat suit, spitting, laxatives, water pills or going without water.** Missing by a few hundred grams costs a fine or a catchweight. Dehydrating at 45, after weeks of cutting, costs the fight and can cost a lot more.
- **After the weigh-in:** the menu's FIGHT DAY plan, with the litres worked out from your fight-week Sunday average.

### Weighing in on the day

- **Monday up to the two days before the fight:** your last step.
- **The two days before the fight:** the full day, Step 0, in low-fibre form. You fight on what's in the tank, so the carbs come back and only the fibre goes. The low-fibre options are:
  - **Before the session:** A, C
  - **Breakfast:** D, E
  - **Mid-morning and lunch:** D without the mushrooms and passata, F, G
  - **Three o'clock:** A, B, D
  - **The load:** A, B, E with the potatoes peeled
  - **Dinner:** A; B, C and E without the mushrooms and passata; D without the mushrooms; F
  - **Bedtime:** any
  - **Weekend post-session:** A, C
- **Fight morning:** wake, toilet, scales. Nothing to eat until you've weighed in; sip water. Then the menu's FIGHT DAY plan.

## AFTER THE FIGHT

The weight plan switches off and the transition menu runs from the next day. **Expect 2–4 kg back within the week.** That's the food, the water and the stored carbohydrate coming back. It's the plan working, not fat.

## WHAT NEVER HAPPENS

- **No water cuts:** no drinking less, no sauna or hot bath to make weight, no sweat suit, no spitting, no water pills, no laxatives, no skipped feeds.
- **No extra training to make weight.** The sessions run exactly as written.
- **Nothing below Step 4.**

## THE ARITHMETIC — FOR THE APP

- **C** (ceiling) = limit + tolerance.
- **F** (fight-week aim) and **M** (weigh-in morning aim) come from the weigh-in table above.
- **U** = natural weight; if blank, U = S.
- **Morning weights** are entered to 0.1 kg. **The average** on any day is the mean of the weights logged that day and the six days before; it needs at least 3. With fewer, the Sunday check asks for one weight and uses that.
- **S** = the average on the day the plan is set. If there isn't one, S is the weight entered that day. The line is then provisional: the first Sunday with an average redraws it from that average with the Sundays left, sets the step from the redrawn line, and decides nothing else.
- **The last check** is the Sunday before the Monday of fight week. For a fight early in the week whose light days would fall before that Sunday, it's the last Sunday before the light days.
- **N** = the number of Sundays after the day the plan is set, up to and including the last check.
- **E** (easy kilos) = the larger of 0 and S − the larger of U and F, at most 0.04 × S.
- **R** (real kilos) = the larger of 0 and S − F − E.
- **d** = R ÷ N, at most 0.01 × U.
- **aim on Sunday k** = S − (the smaller of E and 1.5 × k) − d × k, for k = 1 … N. Show it to 0.1; compare unrounded.
- **Hold:** if S ≤ F, every aim is F and the plan starts at Step 0.
- **First step:** hold → 0; R = 0 → 1; d ≤ 0.35 → 1; d ≤ 0.55 → 2; d ≤ 0.75 → 3; otherwise 4.
- **Forecast** = C − 1.0 + (P − F), shown as "about X". P is the projected fight-week average: the last aim + (the latest average − the latest aim). On the day the plan is set, P is the last aim, or S on hold. Over C shows red; between C − 0.5 and C, amber; otherwise green.
- **The Sunday check**, for k = 1 … N − 1. Here A = this Sunday's average, gap = A − aim, fell = (the smaller of last Sunday's average, or S on the first check, and U) − A, and age = whole weeks since the plan was set. Steps run 0–4.
  1. gap > 0.4 → **BEHIND**. Step + 1, unless A ≤ U and fell > 0.01 × U, or there were 3 or more yellow or red mornings Monday to Sunday, or the step is already 4 — then hold.
  2. Otherwise, if A ≤ U, fell > 0.01 × U and age ≥ 2 → **TOO FAST**: step − 1.
  3. Otherwise, if gap < −0.8, A ≤ U and age ≥ 2 → **AHEAD**: step − 1.
  4. Otherwise → **ON TRACK**: hold.
- **The over-the-ceiling card** shows on the day the plan is set (or redrawn) and at **the checkpoint**, Sunday k = N − 2, whenever the forecast is over C.
- **On Sunday N:** report only. The step doesn't change.
- **Redrawing** after any change to the limit, tolerance, natural weight, weigh-in or fight date: S = the latest average, N = the Sundays left. The step is kept.
